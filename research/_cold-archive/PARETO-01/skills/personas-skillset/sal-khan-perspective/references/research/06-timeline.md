## Key intellectual / strategic arc

1. **From explanation to mastery diagnostics (2004–15).** Remote tutoring exposed prerequisite gaps; recorded lessons solved scheduling, then exercises/personalized practice and the SAT partnership made the unit of intervention the learner’s gap, not the calendar.
2. **From platform to learning ecology (2014–23).** Khan Lab School, Khan World School, and Schoolhouse test the claim that effective education needs relationships, live help, and authentic school conditions—not just more content.
3. **From content scale to AI-enabled personal support (2023–present).** Khanmigo and Writing Coach operationalize a Socratic/teacher-assistant framing. This is a stated aspiration, not proof that it reliably produces one-to-one tutoring outcomes at all scales.
4. **From K–12 access to credentials/workforce (2025–26).** Ohio discussions and the Khan TED Institute point to competency, communication, and AI literacy as an emerging strategy for postsecondary and work; results remain prospective.

## Date/attribution cautions

- Khan Academy’s public pages say Khan Academy was started in **2005**, while its own detailed history says tutoring began **August 2004**, YouTube lessons began **2006**, and nonprofit incorporation occurred **2008**. These describe distinct stages rather than necessarily a contradiction; use the stage-specific date.
- Schoolhouse calls Khan both “founder” and “co-founder” depending on the page. The fuller history names Mehrotra and other early collaborators, so “co-founded” is more precise.
- Current scale metrics change frequently and are organization-reported. They demonstrate stated reach, not independently audited educational impact.
- No event later than 2026-04-14 was located in this research pass; the timeline is current through the stated research date but does not claim there were no later events.
