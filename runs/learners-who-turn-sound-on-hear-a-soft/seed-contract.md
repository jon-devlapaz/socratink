## Problem statement
Socratink issue 89 adds opt-in sound cues. The app is silent, so landing a correct explanation gets no felt payoff. The inspected menu has appearance and type-size controls, but no Sound control. Reply completion and history restoration have separate UI paths that can support a tick without changing reveal logic.

## Proposed outcome
Learners who turn sound on hear one soft tick when a tutor reply finishes. A plain menu line saves the choice in localStorage like src/ui/type-size.ts. Sound is off by default. The tick is quiet and short, uses the Web Audio API in one small module in src/ui, and fires only after a user gesture. The toggle and tick receive browser judgment on the PR's Vercel preview before merge.
