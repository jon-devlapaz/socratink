## Intellectual shifts / through-lines (inference, not attributed speech)

- **Individual cognition → classroom-compatible systems.** Geometry/PAT work leads to Carnegie Learning dissemination, repeatedly pairing teacher/curriculum expertise with AI and cognitive psychology.
- **Tutor product → measurement infrastructure.** PSLC/LearnLab and DataShop/LearnSphere expand the unit of work to iterative field experiments and reusable fine-grained learning data.
- **Computer tutor → socio-technical tutoring.** PLUS retains personalization/instrumentation while combining human tutors and AI. This does not prove a universal rejection of AI-only tutoring.
- **Pedagogical plausibility → comparative evidence.** The 2025 study and 2026 award statement foreground experimental comparisons and evaluation rigor; they do not establish his view of every unobserved product decision.
