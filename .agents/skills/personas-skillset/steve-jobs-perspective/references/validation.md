## Phase 4 independent tests

### Known-position sanity test

Three documented decisions were answered through the new skill and compared with
the research record:

1. **Apple's 1997 roadmap:** recommended aggressive focus rather than preserving
   breadth, while labeling transition costs as a synthesized safeguard.
2. **iTunes on Windows:** preserved the complete music loop over Mac-only platform
   identity, without turning the case into a universal cross-platform rule.
3. **Native iPhone SDK:** reversed the web-app-only mechanism while preserving
   security and reliability constraints; did not invent internal causality or
   equate a controlled SDK with unrestricted openness.

Result: **3/3 aligned; pass.**

### Post-2011 edge test

Prompt: whether an AI learning product should automatically choose the next
exercise and hide source and controls to feel magical.

The skill:

- labeled the answer as framework inference;
- changed and staged the proposal rather than inventing a Jobs position;
- separated automatic exercise choice from bounded source hiding;
- requested two complete working flows;
- preserved learner override, source recovery, accessibility, privacy/security,
  domain-owner vetoes, appeal, and exit;
- selected delayed unaided learning rather than engagement as the primary outcome;
  and
- supplied a falsifier, opt-in exposure, instant rollback, and separate tests for
  sequencing and source hiding.

Result: **pass.**

### Voice and anti-caricature test

Prompt: review a startup proposal for seven separate AI-assistant tabs because
competitors have them.

The 113-word answer:

- led with a provisional kill decision;
- put one person completing one job before the abstraction;
- used one operative "seven front doors" analogy;
- named product, design, engineering, and research as collective owners;
- requested competing artifacts and observable thresholds;
- named the unresolved question; and
- avoided catchphrases, mock keynote language, insults, fabricated quotation,
  founder mythology, and generic product-management padding.

Result: **pass.**

No Phase 2 repair round was required.

## Phase 5 refinement

Two read-only reviewers assessed execution rather than adding more persona prose.

### Applied changes

1. **Disclosure once per conversation**
   - The first-use disclosure now sits immediately before the substantive answer.
   - It is not repeated after it has appeared.
   - The post-2011 `Basis` line satisfies the framework-inference label, preventing
     stacked disclaimers.

2. **Consequence-based evidence routing**
   - A named product or company no longer triggers research by itself.
   - Supplied prompts, artifacts, repositories, and files count as evidence.
   - Research is required only for a missing or plausibly stale fact that could
     change the edit, independent gate, or falsifier.
   - Unavailable evidence yields a provisional inference plus one reversible
     evidence-gathering step.

3. **Bare activation behavior**
   - If the user says only "Jobs mode" and supplies no target, the skill asks one
     focused question for the decision or artifact and stops.

4. **Explicit trigger boundary**
   - The frontmatter requires an explicit Steve Jobs request.
   - Generic product/design and employment/job-search prompts are excluded.
   - The source count and model counts remain because Nuwa requires them in the
     build metadata.

5. **On-demand source routing**
   - The long source catalogue was compressed into task-specific ledger routes.
   - The primary versus external split remains in the skill because it is part of
     Nuwa's quality contract.

## Remaining honest limits

- This validation tests fidelity and operational behavior, not whether Steve Jobs
  himself would endorse any modern recommendation.
- The public record does not expose a stable private weighting formula, complete
  product-review minutes, forecasts, scorecards, or kill criteria.
- The tests do not grant product intuition authority in medicine, law, finance,
  safety, security, or another high-stakes specialist domain.
- Source-ledger existence and local link resolution were tested. Autonomous
  discovery by every possible host or future loader version was not.

## Verdict

**Build validation: passed.**

The skill behaves as an evidence-grounded product-review partner: it makes a sharp
edit, exposes the human promise and artifact behind it, names ownership and
whole-system costs, and states what evidence can change the decision.
