## LearnVector: current competitive facts, plans, and non-facts

### Directly established facts (high confidence)

1. **Founded in 2026; Ng is Founder & CEO.** Its own site says this, and Coursera's release/10-Q identify him as founder and leader.
2. **Coursera invested $100m and held 33.33% fully diluted ownership at investment date.** The SEC filing is the strongest source. “One-third” is a snapshot; it is not a promise of permanent ownership or control.
3. **Coursera chair conflict was disclosed and a special committee approved the transaction.** This is a process fact only.
4. **LearnVector is hiring a small, on-site Mountain View team.** Roles explicitly span AI, learning engineering/science, and full-stack product.
5. **Current public roadmap is early 2027.** The company says it will have something to show by then; Coursera describes first product experiences as targeted then.

### Announced thesis / forward-looking plans (not outcomes)

- A trustworthy one-to-one learning guide that plans an individual path, adapts pace/examples/explanations, and remains until mastery.
- Use of agentic AI for human development; a shift from one-to-many courses to one-to-one learning.
- Potential Coursera/Udemy collaboration and potential access to trusted content, enterprise/higher-ed ecosystem, and learning data.
- Learner engagement framing: “labor to love” and “occasional to daily.”

### Not public or not verified in this pass

- No public product/demo, pricing, learner cohort, retention/transfer study, accuracy/evaluator design, safety evaluation, privacy terms, content-license agreement, data-sharing agreement, commercial contract, customer count, revenue, or launch date beyond the early-2027 target was located.
- No exclusivity or ownership/control rights beyond Coursera's disclosed 33.33% fully diluted stake were established.
- No evidence supports claims that LearnVector already has Coursera/Udemy content/data access, a released agent, demonstrated mastery gains, or a moat beyond announced intent and the disclosed strategic relationship.

**Competitive implication.** `[Inference]` A serious competitor should treat LearnVector's *announced* thesis and strategic assets as hypotheses to monitor, rather than shadow-box a presumed product. The addressable present competitor is the learner's existing workflow/alternative. Any claim of outperforming LearnVector before a comparable product and learner outcome data exist must be phrased as a predeclared experimental ambition, not a demonstrated fact.
